# Original technique families -- 1.12.0-preview

Twenty original families add 120 moves. Each has a grade-4 primer and defense, a grade-3 movement skill, two grade-2 pressure/control skills and a grade-1 payoff. They use actual damage, status, summons and collision-checked movement.

Primers stack up to three marks per enemy for eight seconds and build resonance up to eight. Finishers consume that family marks and resonance. Other players do not share the marks. Guards affect three hits, last a bounded time and spend CE per hit; they cannot grant immunity. Movement vows and healing vows apply.

## Shatterglass (original)

Glass marks crack into additional CE-draining shards when shattered.

- **Splinter Needle** -- Aim at one enemy to add a mark (three layers, eight seconds) and one resonance.
- **Prism Mantle** -- Guard three hits at 20% base reduction (output scales it up to 35%), spending CE on each; family-specific utility also applies.
- **Refraction Step** -- Move along a collision-checked path up to six blocks and gain one resonance.
- **Shard Squall** -- Strike enemies in the aimed cone; each hit applies this family's secondary mechanic.
- **Tessellation Cage** -- Bind visible enemies briefly at the aimed point; existing marks extend the short bind.
- **Catastrophic Shatter** -- Consume marks and stored resonance for a stronger area strike. An unprepared finisher is much weaker.

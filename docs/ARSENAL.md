# Personal arsenals — 1.8.0-preview

Twelve slots are available at every grade. A power occupies one slot, and its mechanical
expression/vows are saved per player. Three banks of four keep the on-screen hotbar compact.

## Binding vows

The workshop's Vows I and Vows II pages provide ten explicit restrictions. Forsaking
ordinary combat gives the selected power x5 output. Open guard, no dashes, one power only,
stationary casting, no RCT, half-health casting, added incantation, tripled recovery and
doubled CE each have their own output bonus. Hover for exact numbers. Combined bonus is
capped at x12, in addition to ordinary output tuning. Restrictions on ordinary actions
apply while the vowed power remains learned, even if it is not equipped.

Casting a vowed power seals vow edits for at least 60 game seconds (longer for lasting summons). The seal is saved through death
and relog. New characters clear it. Switching slots cannot remove a restriction. An ordinary
weapon attack cannot bypass the combat vow by disabling Cursed Combat. A single-power vow
forbids all other technique casts and cannot be promised by two powers at once. Incompatible
vows are unavailable, such as promising no dashes on the dash power itself.

## New families

- Electrification: Charge Transfer, Discharge, Return Stroke. Charge marks belong to their
  caster and are consumed by discharge; unmarked enemies cannot be discharged.
- Granite Blast: Granite Blast, Barrage, Full Discharge. Concentrated CE projectiles vary in
  number, piercing, blast area and cost.
- Love Rendezvous: Star Mark, Attraction, Repulsion. This adaptation uses private constellation
  marks to control which enemies can be pulled or repelled.
- Explosive Flesh: Tooth Shot, Eye Bomb, Detonation. Gameplay adaptation using CE rather than
  permanently removing body parts.
- Tool Manipulation: Wind Scythe, Aerial Lift, Gale Cutter. Wind arcs, lift and cutting projectiles.
- Echo Ledger (original): First Impression records an echo and return point; Collection consumes
  the marks for damage; Reprise returns to the recorded position if nearby and unobstructed.
- Mercury Mirror (original): Catch absorbs part of direct incoming damage; Return releases stored
  force; Silver Rain fires editable mirror shards. Domain sure-hits bypass Catch.
- Mourning Thread (original): Stitch binds a target, Sever cuts along a narrow line, Funeral Loom
  traps a nearby group. Its theme is cursed funeral thread, with adjustable binding/aim/force.

All eight families participate in Random awakening and the Creative picker. Their 24 powers
also have ordinary shapeless scroll recipes and grade gates. The full roster is 95 powers in
29 families. Original techniques are labeled in their descriptions; named canon concepts are
Minecraft gameplay adaptations, not reproductions of every manga condition or feat.

Canon concept reference: [official anime character profiles](https://jujutsukaisen.jp/character/?category=jujutsu).
The original families and low-grade creatures were created for this mod; no fan artwork or
other creators' technique descriptions are copied.

## Curse ecology

Twelve new Grade 4/3 species bring the roster to 52. They have dedicated geometry, native UV
art, combat clips, spawn eggs and loot. Natural checks occur every six seconds with a 40%
base encounter roll, compared with the former two-second/65% roll. At most one group is
created per successful player check. Grade 4 packs have 2-4 members, Grade 3/semi-Grade 2
packs have 2-3, and Grade 2+ appears singly with a 24-block exclusion around other curses.
Weak packs may mix species of the same grade. Strong grades also receive lower pick weights.
Existing configuration multipliers and local budgets still apply.

# Playing 1.12.0-preview

For chargeable moves, hold R or a direct power key, then release to cast. Three seconds reaches 100%;
Hollow Purple reaches 200% after seven seconds. Watch the CE/s meter: long holds are
expensive, especially without Six Eyes. Other moves activate on press: summons, swaps,
movement, melee and utility do not charge. Infinity toggles on press; healing/guard
channels start on press and stop on release. Tooltips and the loadout show the casting
style. Opening a screen cancels charging or held channels. See [charging and terrain](CHARGING.md).

Auto Combat is ON by default. Hold M1 while in combat mode to let it choose simple attacks
and defensive responses. It reacts conservatively and attacks more slowly than precise manual
play; it does not choose manual double-tap specials or evasive escapes for you. Toggle it in
J -> Moves -> Bindings, the mod config, or F7. Your saved preference survives restarting.
Auto commits three of every five opportunities and performs visible feints on the others;
moving targets and guards can lower its actual contact rate. Only manual guard can parry
perfectly. The combat HUD shows observed contact accuracy after five attempts.

Craft the new armor and weapons through vanilla recipes. Uniform pieces reduce technique
CE costs; Binding Vestments spend CE for bounded protection; Heavenly Hunter pieces improve
physical mobility. Held tools with granted techniques also cast on Use. See
[equipment, visuals and optional rendering mods](VISUAL_OVERHAUL.md).

Y toggles combat; Backslash changes the selected power bank.

Manual defaults: U light hit, double-U uppercut; I heavy hit, double-I sweep; hold Right Bracket (]) to block.
Crouching blocks low sweeps. Raise guard just before contact for a parry. Left Alt dashes;
while staggered it spends 33 CE on an escape with a ten-second cooldown. All are rebindable.
Q still drops, E opens inventory and F swaps hands. Normal number keys still select inventory.
Direct power keys default to Numpad 1-4, operating on the selected bank. R casts the selected
power; V cycles all twelve slots; hold G and scroll to select a slot. The bank/toggle-combat keys are listed
in Bindings. Players without a keypad can bind their preferred unused keys or use R/G.

All twelve slots are usable from awakening. Click a learned move, then a destination slot;
right-click clears a slot, and cleared slots can be refilled. Each move occupies one slot.
Drag-and-drop and the explicit Equip button also work. Hover the right-hand area for details.

Personalize is independent for every learned move and player. The workshop has ten pages:
Core, Delivery, Control, Trajectory, Form, Summons, Trade-offs, Style, Vows I and Vows II.
The first eight retain their numeric controls, custom name, RGB color and trails. The final
two add ten vows. Hover each vow to see its exact restriction and output bonus, then Apply.
Forsake combat grants x5 output for that power and prevents ordinary attacks even when it is
unslotted or combat mode is off. Other vows restrict guard, dash, other powers, movement,
health, healing, preparation, recovery or CE spending. Bonuses combine up to x12.
Daily restrictions apply character-wide while you retain the learned power with that vow.
After casting a vowed power, changing vows is sealed for 60 game seconds, including relogs.
Unusable combinations are disabled/rejected. Other numeric tuning remains editable.

Full domains protect their interior from outside attacks until their barrier breaks.
Barrier integrity appears on the domain HUD, with cracks as it weakens. Enough damage during
the hand sign interrupts expansion; sustained damage to the caster collapses an active domain.
Ordinary blocking, parries and dash frames cannot cancel sure-hits. Use Simple Domain or
domain-specific counterplay. Interior scenery is rendered without replacing world blocks.

In Creative, **J → Moves → Creative picker** selects a complete family at
**Calamity grade**, with mastered moves, maximum Growth, its domain, reinforcement and RCT.
The awakening screen also has this shortcut. Survival packets cannot use the grant.
The picker can include Mechamaru's restriction; choosing again replaces the family.

Practice shows independent birth reserves and regeneration level (1–10). Regeneration
potential is rolled once and survives relog, ordinary death and respec. Smaller current
capacities get larger training gains (25–125 base CE) for fewer XP levels (1–30).
The button displays the actual gain/price, which changes as capacity improves.

Mechamaru's restriction can roll among Heavenly Restrictions. It has reduced health and
movement, begins with **zero stored CE**, and can keep accumulating without a gameplay
capacity cap. It permits innate techniques and universal CE skills. Its finite working
potential determines regeneration and percentage costs; it is not a storage limit.
Grade changes and Fill never conjure an unlimited balance. Its HUD displays storage as ∞.

Press **J**, open **Moves**, select a learned move and click **Personalize**.
Adjust applicable controls, inspect the cost preview, then Apply.
Output scales power; Reach scales targeting/teleports; Area scales fields/width;
Duration scales effects; a shorter cooldown costs more CE. Infinity's filter cycles
between projectile protection, contact repulsion and combined protection (extra upkeep).
Each character has separate saved expressions of their learned moves.

Open **Practice** to learn reinforcement (3 vanilla XP levels) or RCT (10 levels).
Reinforcement raises damage and reduces incoming damage while consuming CE; toggle it
there. H toggles learned RCT healing. Output and the healing threshold are adjustable.
Reserve training shows a variable gain and XP price based on current capacity.
Growth's Output and grade multipliers also increase capacity. Heavenly Restrictions
with no CE prevent these skills. Creative mode waives training XP costs. No slots used.

New characters independently roll 100–3000 base CE; the roll does not depend on family.
Existing sorcerers retain their old base. Normal respawn, relog and respec keep birth
potential; exorcism starts a new character with a new roll.

Use F5 to inspect animated punches, hooks, uppercuts, heavy strikes, sweeps and guards.
The server decides contact timing. Powers use casting gestures as well as VFX.

Use a new world, or log back into an existing character: family characters automatically
lose the incorrect generic punch/teleport fillers. Blood Manipulation starts with
Crimson Binding and Blood Needle; Flowing Red Scale and Piercing Blood unlock by grade.

J opens your profile. Techniques lets you equip up to twelve moves; learned moves remain
in the library. The wheel displays twelve slots. A slot may bind a family selector or an individual move. R casts, V changes slot, hold G and scroll to select a slot.
C toggles framed combat; tap left-click for light, hold/release for heavy, right-click
guards, Alt dashes, Z locks on. The Traits tab shows attributes separately from moves.

Normal death offers the ordinary bed/anchor/world respawn, cursed-spirit rebirth, or a
cursed object. Colony reincarnation requires a prior Kenjaku pact and its revival price.
Hardcore death also offers cursed-spirit rebirth, without free bed respawning. Pact holders
can choose their contracted routes. A spirit keeps its technique, has a visible cursed
body in third person, and regenerates wounds by spending CE. Exorcism starts a new human
character with a different technique family, including in Hardcore.

Brain Transplant targets a labeled fallen humanoid body within six blocks. It changes
your rendered host, position, maximum health, and provides up to three host moves beside
Brain Transplant. It consumes the host once; living players cannot be possessed. Use F5
to inspect your body. Fallen hosts last one game day.

Cursed Spirit Manipulation: aim at a curse below 30% health and sneak-cast to absorb it.
Cast normally to release a stored curse. Up to eight may be stored and three released at
once. They follow and fight for you. Maximum Uzumaki consumes the entire stored stock.

M opens the Culling map. Pick a colony and confirm entry to travel there and accept its
barrier rules. Participants can negotiate through their personal Kogane from the map.
The game no longer ends by default merely because the NPC population was reduced.
Normal respawning releases a participant rather than pulling them away from their bed.

Modulo is part of the normal power sandbox. Random awakening can roll Harmony / Chaos or
Light / Darkness, and Creative's picker grants either family at Calamity. Their eight moves
also have craftable scrolls: paper + amethyst shard + one material listed in MODULO.md.
There are no story missions, boarding events, expedition choices or required story dimensions.
Old story players return to their remembered world/respawn point when joining.

Operator testing: /jjk technique grant @s jjkcraft:brain_transplant or another technique
id, then equip it in J. /jjk curse list and /jjk curse spawn <id> provide curse targets.
The dev client's saves live under run/saves. Changes/builds are local, without a GitHub push.

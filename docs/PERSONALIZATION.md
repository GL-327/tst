# Personal technique expressions

1.5 extends expressions with typed projectile delivery, penetration, preparation time,
force, placement and target filters. Additional delivery modifiers multiply the core CE
budget; charge time trades preparation for cost. Names and colors are cosmetic. Cast
contexts snapshot target/placement rules, and VFX packets carry the authoritative color.
Practice independently persists birth regeneration (1–10) and rolled-reserve state.
Reserve training prices/gains use finite working capacity, so draining before training
does not earn a discount. Mechamaru banks bypass storage clamping while keeping a finite
reference for regeneration/percentage costs; regeneration never compounds with bank size.

`TechniqueTuning` stores bounded integer percentages for each known move. The server
rejects unknown moves, normalizes inapplicable axes and copies registry definitions into
immutable cast snapshots. Active channels end at their old cost before editing; pending
casts keep their paid snapshot. Repeated requests cannot stack windups.

Cost scales with output^1.65, reach^0.55, area^0.75, duration^0.45 and cooldown^-0.8,
bounded to 0.15–64 times base cost. Upkeep and Mul costs follow the same budget. Normal
Control/mastery/vow modifiers still apply. The preview includes Control, mastery and
Six Eyes; situational vows or tool cost payers can change the final charge.

Typed behavior copies cover every existing behavior record. Targeting/teleports,
projectile size/lifetime, summon lifetime/power, field forces/radii, binding duration,
healing and Infinity attenuation use the chosen values. Body transfer keeps corpse and
line-of-sight checks with an 18-block ceiling. Defaults preserve data-pack definitions.

Birth potential is a separate persistent attachment, independently rolled before family
selection. Smaller pools are more common; both 100 and 3000 are possible. Existing awakened
saves retain their base. Training is bounded to +10000 base CE and spends XP levels.
Respec preserves potential/training; cursed-spirit exorcism resets character potential.

Reinforcement consumes CE every ten ticks; bonuses require a paid beat. RCT spending
scales with healing output and keeps an 8% reserve buffer. Zero-CE restrictions apply.

Combat and armor poses use authoritative startup/contact/recovery frames, synchronize to
observers, and mirror left-handed arms. First-person transforms and category-specific cast
gestures supplement these poses without deciding damage or cooldowns.

The workshop edits each technique's existing mechanics. It does not create arbitrary
scripted behaviors. Human PvP balance, weapon-specific choreography and third-party
player-model animation compatibility still need playtesting.

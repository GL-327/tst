## Original local pass

For the reconciled 1.12.2 build, GitHub's creature rigs and paired atlases are the active
creature assets. These source sheets preserve the earlier local art work. The combined
runtime uses the local equipment sprites for unique retained items, the Ritual armour
atlases, and the painted effect atlas for distant impacts. Earlier asset counts and bake
instructions below describe the original local pass, not the entire combined build.

All three source sheets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. Runtime assets are saved under `src/main/resources/assets/jjkcraft/textures/`. The source sheets remain in this repository; the mod does not depend on the Codex image cache.

The material bake uses the frozen original UV/silhouette textures in `base-textures.zip` (repository revision f1c56ba), samples painted material detail, and preserves entity/block alpha masks. Model rigs and UV coordinates remain compatible. `tools/art_overhaul.py` splits the equipment sheet into separate 128px inventory sprites, paints native worn armour UVs, and packs the effect sheet into a padded 1024px atlas.

## Design reference

The public 01 Studios Jujutsu Playground listing describes an ability picker, hotkeys, and an abilities menu with per-ability options: https://www.nexusmods.com/peopleplayground/mods/148?tab=description . The creator's public patch notes also mention settings inside the abilities menu: https://01studio.dev/blog/jujutsu-playground-95-patch-note/ . These inform the grouping/interaction pattern. This implementation uses this Minecraft mod's own mechanics, networking, art and code; it is not a port of the other mod's implementation.
